About WiLO

Our mission

Women in Lasers & Optics is a registered student organization at the University of Central Florida, based at CREOL, The College of Optics and Photonics. We are dedicated to empowering and platforming women and other underrepresented groups in optics through professional development seminars, technical workshops, and community events.

Membership is open to every UCF student, postdoc and staff member who shares that goal. You don't need to be in an optics program to join.

About the Summit

Breaking the mold

This year’s theme, Breaking the Mold, puts the spotlight on the different journeys and passions that led each speaker to success.

Talks mix technical and professional topics in a roughly 60/40 split. The point of that split is to remind each other that we are more than our technical work, and that key parts of success happen outside the lab.

The day opens with a coffee chat, runs through six talks and lunch, and wraps up with a discussion-focused Q&A panel.

Professional focus topics
Life
Working with life: family, job loss, relocation, the two-body problem
Mentorship
Finding and developing mentorship relationships
Advancement
Negotiation tactics as a woman in STEM
Transitions
Moving from academia to industry
Network
Making and maintaining a network to thrive in your field

Outreach

Sharing the light

WiLO members bring hands-on optics into classrooms, libraries and science nights across Central Florida. Our goal is simple: let students see a laser up close, split white light into a rainbow, and meet women who do this for a living.

400 nmvisible700 nm
Program · Classrooms

K–12 school visits

Volunteers lead 45-minute demo sessions: total internal reflection in a water stream, diffraction-grating glasses, and polarization with sunglasses and an LCD screen.

Program · Community

Science nights & festivals

A WiLO table at community STEM events, with take-home spectroscopes and laser-maze challenges for all ages.

Program · On campus

CREOL lab tours

Guided tours for visiting middle and high school groups, hosted by graduate students in their own research labs.

Get in touch

Request a visit

Teachers and event organizers in the Orlando area can request a WiLO demo. Tell us your grade level, group size and preferred dates, and we'll match you with volunteers.

Members who want to volunteer can sign up at any general body meeting. No outreach experience needed; we train you on every demo.
